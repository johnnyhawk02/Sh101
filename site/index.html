    Shyoy v0.22.9

    VCO. An exponential converter, whose thermal voltage follows temperature, sets the current charging a 1 nF timing cap. A comparator at 5 V fires a reset transistor that discharges the cap through its on-resistance for a set pulse width. The pulse wave is a second comparator against the PW reference. Edges are band-limited with sub-sample polyBLEP.

    Sub-oscillator. Two flip-flops clocked by the VCO reset give a square one octave down, a square two octaves down and a 25% pulse two octaves down. Their level follows the logic rail.

    Mixer. An inverting op-amp summer that soft-clips at the supply rails, then a coupling cap.

    VCF. Four OTA-C integrator stages, each I = Iabc·tanh(ΔV/2Vt) into its own 240 pF cap, with resonance feedback from stage 4 through a diode clamp. Solved per sample as a zero-delay (TPT) network, re-linearised around each OTA's operating point.

    VCA. An OTA with a tanh input, its Iabc set by the envelope or the gate.

    Envelope. A 10 µF timing cap charged toward the rail through the attack resistor. A comparator at about 4.3 V hands over to decay, which discharges toward the sustain voltage.

    LFO. An op-amp integrator and Schmitt trigger relaxation oscillator on a 0.1 µF cap. Random samples noise on each square edge.

    Power and battery sag. A 9 V battery with internal resistance feeds the supply. Its voltage sags under load, and the rails start to fall once less than about 6 V reaches the converter. The header shows the battery voltage and the rail.

    Accent. A 303-style sweep capacitor: consecutive accents build up the filter sweep. Acc adds accent to the cutoff (0 is the real 101).

    Bend board. The VCO timing cap, reset pulse width and discharge resistance, each VCF stage cap, OTA offset, resonance polarity, envelope cap and attack threshold, LFO cap and hysteresis, temperature, battery voltage and internal resistance.

    Bridges. Any tap node (saw cap, pulse comparator, sub flip-flop, noise, LFO, envelope cap, VCF stage 2, VCF out, VCA out, or a finger with mains hum) to any injection point, through 10 MΩ down to 1 kΩ, applied as current into a cap or summing node.

    RAT. An LM308 stage with gain-bandwidth and slew limits from its 30 pF cap, 1N914 diodes to ground, then the 3.3 nF filter. Run at 4× the host rate.

    DS-1 and TS808. The Boss DS-1 (transistor booster, op-amp into 1N4148 diodes to ground, tone filter) and the Ibanez TS808 (JRC4558 with diodes in the feedback, tone stage), from ElectroSmash's part values, at 4×.

    Folder. The Buchla 259 timbre wavefolder after Esqueda, Pöntynen, Välimäki and Parker (DAFx-17): five folding cells on 6 V rails, level-matched by a peak follower.

    Flanger. After the Boss BF-2: a 1024-stage bucket brigade clocked from 500 kHz down to 40 kHz (1 to 12.8 ms, swept as 1/f by a triangle LFO), Res feedback through a soft clip, 8 kHz filters either side, the dry and delayed signals mixed equally. Chorus mode has no feedback.

    Routing. The strip under the units: the folder before or after the drive, and the delay and reverb side by side or one into the other (the second fed the dry signal plus the first one's return). Click a block to switch its unit.

    Delay. A clean digital delay synced to the tempo, ping-pong or mono, with a low cut and a high cut inside the feedback loop so each repeat gets thinner and darker.

    Reverb slot. Four units: a Lexicon 224 (20 kHz, 12-bit gain-ranging converters, 16-bit words, 32K-word memory, on Dattorro's Griesinger-style plate; the variants are estimates), Dattorro's plate (JAES 1997), a spring tank after Välimäki, Parker and Abel (JAES 2010), and partitioned FFT convolution.

    Convolution IRs. EMT 140 plate impulse responses by Greg Hopkins (Hopkins Media Services), Creative Commons Attribution, via oramics/sampled. Drum room, silo, garage, Musikverein and On a Star impulse responses from Voxengo, free for any use. See irs/CREDITS.md.

    Compactor. A sidechain on the delay and reverb returns, keyed by the dry synth, after the Kilohearts Compactor: Amount sets depth and sensitivity, Release 0 to 1 s, with 3 ms lookahead.
