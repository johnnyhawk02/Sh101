Bend board

Swap component values on the live circuit, or bridge any circuit node into another through a resistor. Lower resistance means a stronger bridge. Double-click any control to put it back to stock.

Battery 9.0 V · rail 100%

Bridges

Rows are nodes you can tap; columns are where the wire lands. Your finger carries mains hum, like touching the board.

Step sequencer

Drag a note up or down to change pitch. Click a step number, then play a key to write it in. Ties hold the previous note; slides glide into the next step without retriggering.

Seq 0/100

Play from your computer keyboard: A–K white keys, W E T Y U black keys, Z/X transpose low/high, Space run/stop. While a sequencer runs, keys transpose it. Arpeggio and the 100-step sequencer step once per LFO cycle, so the LFO rate is their tempo. In Load, each key adds a step; play the next key before releasing the last to tie them. Sound starts on your first click or key press. Your patch and pattern are kept in this browser.
